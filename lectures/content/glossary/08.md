| $z$ | The distance of a value from the mean in units of $s$: $z = (x - \bar{x})/s$. | The Gaussian Check in Numbers |
| central limit theorem | Means of many independent values come out Gaussian, whatever the values look like. | Means of 100 Random Hours: a Gaussian |
| covariance | How two columns move together: $s_{xy} = \frac{1}{N-1} \sum_{i=1}^{N} (x_i - \bar{x})(y_i - \bar{y})$. | Covariance: Do Two Columns Move Together? |
| correlation coefficient $r$ | How closely two columns follow a straight line, from −1 to +1: $r = s_{xy}/(s_x\, s_y)$. | The Correlation Coefficient r |
| standard error | How far a mean of $N$ independent values typically lies from the long-run mean: $s/\sqrt{N}$. | The Variance of a Mean |
| significant digits | The digits from the first non-zero one; a ± keeps two: 0.0299096 → 0.030. | Writing a Result with Its ± |
| Bayes' rule | Turns a conditional probability around: $P(B \mid A) = P(A \mid B)\,P(B)/P(A)$. | Bayes' Rule |
